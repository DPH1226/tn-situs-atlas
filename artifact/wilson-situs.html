01

What this run proves

Before Civvix tells a Finance Director that the Department of Revenue has miscoded a business, it should be able to show that the geography underneath the claim is sound. This page is that proof, and it doubles as a working exception queue. Every rooftop in Wilson County has been placed inside the Department's own tax-rate polygon, measured against every jurisdictional seam in feet, and cross-checked against three independent official layers.

02

The boundary-risk surface

distance to nearest jurisdictional seam

A seam is where two different situs codes meet — not merely where a polygon ends. Wilson County has of them. The bands below are the control: what a location is close to determines how much human judgment its situs assignment requires.

03

Wilson County, coded

Four situs codes cover the county. The heavy lines are county seams — a business miscoded across one of those costs Wilson both halves of the local option tax. The lighter lines are city seams, which cost only the situs half. Points are the public business universe, rooftop-placed by the county's 911 district.

06

Where the data came from, and what it cost

Wilson County offers six GIS layers at $75 each — $450. This run used none of them. Address points, road-level geography, municipal limits and the authoritative tax boundary are all published free by the State of Tennessee, by the county's own 911 district, and by the City of Mt. Juliet. The county's parcel layer remains the one genuine purchase candidate, and only if a specific exception turns on parcel geometry.

07

What is waiting on the Department of Revenue

Ready to send

  • Written request under T.C.A. § 67-1-1704(d) for the Wilson County situs report, covering all four situs codes — 9500, 9501, 9502, 9503.
  • Contractor designation under § 67-1-1704(e), limited on its face to name, address and situs and nothing else.
  • A 35-ZIP request universe: 14 ZIPs wholly or partly in Wilson, 21 in the surrounding ring, each with its own written justification.
  • Reconciliation request for the City/County Detail and Month Detail reports from REP.

What arrives with it

  • The roster joins to this surface on business name and address. Every row already has a rooftop, a situs, a seam distance and an evidence score waiting for it.
  • The comparison is then mechanical: coded situs versus measured situs.
  • The one-year correction window means every month before the first submission is unrecoverable. That is arithmetic, not salesmanship.

Prepared by Civvix Inc. for Aaron Maynard, Finance Director, Wilson County, Tennessee. Prototype — not an audit finding. No confidential tax information was requested, received or used in producing this page. Business locations come from the Wilson County Emergency Communications District's public label layer, which carries a business name and a rooftop coordinate but no address, NAICS code or licence linkage; names are matched to the nearest public address point within 500 feet. Map geometry is simplified for display only — all measurement was performed on full-precision geometry in EPSG:2274.
