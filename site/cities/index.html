How to read this. Cross-county postal and boundary risk are businesses inside the city polygon flagged as on the county page. Postal exposure is a business outside the polygon carrying this city's mailing address — where a mailing-address registration would have coded it to the city. File says elsewhere is the city's counter-finding: rooftops inside the polygon that the State's own address-range file codes to another jurisdiction, usually the county's unincorporated code; corrected, those move money to the city. File claims city is the reverse — rooftops outside the polygon the file codes to the city. Other layers say city is the strongest counter-finding: rooftops the Department places outside the city while both the 911 authority and the Comptroller's certified limits place them inside it. None of these is a finding until the city's situs report is compared.
