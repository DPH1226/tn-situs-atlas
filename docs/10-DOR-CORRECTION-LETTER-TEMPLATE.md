# Situs correction request to the Tennessee Department of Revenue — template

*Tennessee publishes no form and no sample letter. This template is synthesized from the only
documented Tennessee procedure (MTAS-544: mark corrections on the situs report and send it to
Financial Control with account numbers) and from the states that do publish a form — California's
CDTFA-549 petition and Regulation 35056 (the seven required elements and the "sufficient factual data"
standard), Alabama's Local Account Number Correction form (incorrect code / correct code / effective
date, signed by an authorized official), and Illinois' referral statute (account ID, location, and
"the specific reason … including as much detail as possible"). It is written to satisfy the strictest
of those so that a Tennessee reviewer has nothing to send back.*

## Notes on use

- **The notification date is the money.** The Department corrects one year back from notification
  (MTAS; practice, not statute). State the date in the subject line and in ¶5, send by a method that
  records receipt, and ask the Department to confirm the date it has logged. California protects the
  "date of knowledge" for a petition that is later supplemented within 30 days; Tennessee has no
  such rule, so file complete.
- **One account per packet; one letter per batch.** A reviewer should be able to verify any single
  account without reading the others.
- **Account numbers come from the situs report** and appear only in the letter and packets that the
  county sends. They never appear in anything the contractor retains.
- **Sign as the local government.** The contractor prepares; the authorized official signs and
  sends. This matches Knox County's 2020 structure and keeps the § 67-1-1704(e) non-disclosure
  question from ever arising.
- **Expect the "verification" step.** Per Knox County's RFP, the Department asks the other party to
  concur, not reply (treated as concurrence), or dispute. Build the calendar for it.
